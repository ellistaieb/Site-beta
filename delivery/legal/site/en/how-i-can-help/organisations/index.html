A few helpful answers

Do I need to know exactly what I need?

No. You can begin with a situation, a question or a sense that something is difficult. We will clarify your needs together.

Which tools will you use?

The choice depends on your needs and the agreed framework. No technique is used automatically.

How many sessions should I expect?

We agree the pace and number of sessions together, and review them as we go. The programmes shown are indicative.
