An approach shaped
around your story.

Everyone brings a different story. My approach begins with listening, understanding your needs and agreeing a framework that feels comfortable. The tools evolve with our work, in conversation with you.

Tools shaped around your needs

The choice of tools depends on your situation, preferences and the agreed framework. We take time to discuss it together.

01

Hypnotherapy

An approach drawing on attention and imagination to explore different ways of experiencing a situation.

02

Brief therapies

Work focused on a current difficulty, your resources and the changes you would like to consider.

03

CBT-informed approaches

Ways to observe connections between thoughts, feelings and behaviours, and try different responses.

04

Neurofeedback

A method providing feedback on recorded brain activity. Its suitability and practical arrangements are discussed within the proposed framework.

05

Coaching

Space to clarify goals, recognise resources and put decisions into action.

This support does not replace medical care when it is needed.
